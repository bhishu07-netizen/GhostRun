Who can play

You must be 13 or older. If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission to play.

Play safe

Ghostrun is played on foot, outdoors, often while running. You play at your own risk, and you're responsible for your own safety and the safety of people around you.

  • Look up from your phone. Never play while driving, cycling or crossing a road.
  • Follow traffic rules and watch out for vehicles, stairs, water and uneven ground.
  • Don't run where it's crowded or where you could knock someone over.
  • Don't play in the dark or in bad weather if it isn't safe to.
  • Stop if you feel unwell, and look out for the other players.

Respect places and people

  • Only go where you're allowed to be. Don't enter private property, locked or restricted areas, or anywhere a campus, landowner or security guard has told you not to.
  • Follow the rules of the place you're playing in, like your campus, a park or a building.
  • Don't hide in dangerous spots, such as on roofs, near machinery, in vehicles or near water.
  • "Catching" means a light tag. Never grab, push, trip or chase anyone who isn't playing.

Photo hints

  • Photo hints should show the place around you. Keep people's faces out of the shot, especially strangers.
  • Don't send anything rude, violent or sexual, anything illegal, or anything that shows someone's private information.
  • You're responsible for the photos you send.

Fair play and good behaviour

  • Be honest when someone catches you. Confirm a real tag and don't reject it to stay in.
  • Pick a name that isn't offensive and doesn't pretend to be someone else.
  • Don't use Ghostrun to stalk, follow, harass or find anyone. Only play with people who agreed to play.
  • Don't try to break, overload or misuse the game, or read other players' data.

We may remove games or players that break these rules.
