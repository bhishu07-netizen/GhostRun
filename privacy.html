Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version. Ghostrun has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Your live location goes only to the players in your game, and only while the game is running. Locations and photo hints are deleted the moment a game ends. Everything else about a game is deleted within 24 hours.

Who we are

Ghostrun is a free, real-life hide-and-seek game made by Bhavishay Mehendiratta. In this policy, "we" means the person who runs Ghostrun. Contact: %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL%.

What we collect, and why

  • Name and avatar. The name you type and the avatar you pick, so other players know who you are. There's no account and no password.
  • Live location. Your GPS position and its accuracy, sent about every 5 seconds while a game is live. We keep only your latest position, and only to run the game.
  • Play zone, if the host sets one. The host's position when they draw the zone becomes its centre. Everyone in that game sees the zone on their map.
  • Photo hints. When you're a ghost and a ping asks for a hint, you take one photo of the place around you. It is sent only when you tap "Send hint".
  • Game data. Your team, the catches you make or confirm, when you were last connected, and the game's code, mode and timers.
  • A notification address, only if you turn on alerts. Your browser gives us an address at its push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) so we can send game alerts, like "Ping in 1 minute". It can only be used to send notifications to this browser.
  • An age check. You choose your birth year on your phone. The year stays on your phone, and our server only learns that you said you're 13 or older.

We don't collect your email, phone number, contacts or real identity. There are no analytics, ads or tracking pixels, and no cookies are used for tracking.

Who can see it

  • Players in your game see your name, avatar and team.
  • Your teammates see your live position on the map.
  • Hunters see a ghost's position only at a scheduled ping, or as a penalty: when a ghost misses a photo hint, or stays outside the play zone for more than 30 seconds. They also see the ghosts' photo hints.
  • Ghosts never see the hunters' positions.
  • Nobody outside your game can see any of it. We never sell or share your data with anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Locations, photo hints and notification addresses are deleted as soon as the game ends. The record of where ghosts were at each ping is wiped at the same time.
  • Alerts are deleted from our server as soon as they're sent.
  • If you leave a game, your player record is deleted right away.
  • Everything else about a game (names, teams, catches, the play zone) is deleted automatically 24 hours after the game was created.

What stays on your phone

Your browser's local storage keeps:

  • your name, avatar and birth year;
  • a random game key that lets you rejoin your game;
  • your last map position, rounded to about 100 metres, so the map opens near you next time.

These never leave your phone, except your name and avatar when you join a game. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Camera and location permissions

Ghostrun asks for location when a game starts, for the camera only when you send a photo hint, and for notifications only if you tap "Turn on alerts". The camera is turned off as soon as the hint is sent or the time runs out. You can turn any of these permissions off in your browser at any time, but the game can't work properly without location.

Services we use

Ghostrun runs on a few outside services, and their servers may be outside your country. They see technical details like your IP address, as any website does:

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Supabase hosts the game database.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles.
  • Esri serves the satellite pictures, only if you switch the map to Satellite.
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers alerts, if you turn them on. Alerts are encrypted, so the push service can't read them.

Fonts and images come from our own site.

Age

Ghostrun is for players aged 13 and older. If you're under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is OK with you playing. If you believe a child under 13 has played, contact us and we'll delete their data. In most cases it will already be gone within 24 hours.

Your choices

You can leave a game at any time from the menu, which deletes your player record. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted sooner. We'll reply as soon as we can.

Changes

If we change how Ghostrun handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
